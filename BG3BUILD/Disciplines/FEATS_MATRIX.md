# 🏅 Feats Matrix: v13.2 High-Fidelity Standard (Patch 8)

This node governs the mechanical triggers that bridge Classes and equipment. It has been forensically satiated with the **Full Pool of 42 Feats** for Gemini Pro build architecture.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### [WIKI_LOCK] Core Feat Rules
> - **Total Pool**: There are 42 distinct feats in BG3.
> - **Availability (SSoT)**: Feats are available at Levels 4, 8, and 12 for all classes (Fighters gain an extra at 6 and 14; Rogues at 10).
> - **Half-Feats**: Feats that provide a +1 to an attribute are critical for 'rounding out' odd-numbered stat arrays.
