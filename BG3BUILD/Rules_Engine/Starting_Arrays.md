### 1. The "Professional" Point-Buy Template
For 100% optimization, characters should use the **17 / 16 / 14 / 10 / 8 / 8** distribution. This maximizes the three most important stats while dumping the irrelevant ones. 

*Note: The "17" is intended to be combined with **Auntie Ethel's Hair** or a **Half-Feat** at Level 4 to reach the 18 threshold.*
